# Archive milestone research (20 reports / ~6 months)

## Is milestone-marking a real practice?

Yes, but the real tradition is the **100th issue**, not 20 or 6 months. Independent
newsletter writers (Substack "100 Newsletters" posts, Fresh van Root, The Revenue
Architect, Gomez Writes) treat issue 100 as meaningful precisely because survival
curves are brutal — most series never reach it, and at weekly cadence 100 issues is
~2 years of consistency. That's the actual signal being marked: persistence, not the
number itself. Milestone-marketing guidance (Substack's own "how to write a great
milestone post," Animalz, Forbes) reinforces the same point: a milestone post works
when it's framed around the reader/subscriber and honest reflection, not a self-
congratulatory vanity beat — several sources explicitly warn against exactly that.

20 reports / 6 months doesn't clear that bar. It's an early-stage checkpoint, not a
survivorship milestone, and ARI3LLA INDEX's voice rules (no hype, no first person,
report/wire-service tone) are a bad fit for a celebratory milestone post regardless of
count. **Recommendation: don't manufacture a 20-report/6-month milestone feature.**
If anything is worth logging, it's an internal changelog note ("archive crossed 20
reports"), not reader-facing copy. Revisit at report 50 or 100, or at the 1-year mark,
where the "sustained a weekly cadence" claim would actually be true and voice-
compatible (stated plainly, not celebrated).
