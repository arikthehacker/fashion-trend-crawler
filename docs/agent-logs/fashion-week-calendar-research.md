Research task, no code changes. Confirms real-world dates for the upcoming

## Confirmed / typical dates

- **New York (NYFW)**: Official CFDA schedule Sept 10–15, 2026 (satellite
  events like The Bureau and Runway7 run roughly Sept 8–13 under the NYFW
  banner). ([CFDA](https://cfda.com/news/view-the-preliminary-september-2026-official-nyfw-schedule/), [The Bureau Fashion Week](https://thebureaufashionweek.com/new-york-fashion-week/september-2026-schedule/))
- **London (LFW)**: Sept 17–21, 2026. ([NationalWorld](https://www.nationalworld.com/lifestyle/fashion/fashion-week-2026-new-york-london-paris-3840512))
- **Milan (MFW)**: Sept 23–29, 2026. ([NationalWorld](https://www.nationalworld.com/lifestyle/fashion/fashion-week-2026-new-york-london-paris-3840512))
- **Paris (PFW)**: Sept 29–Oct 6, 2026. ([NationalWorld](https://www.nationalworld.com/lifestyle/fashion/fashion-week-2026-new-york-london-paris-3840512))

Sequence is consistent year over year: NY → London → Milan → Paris, back-to-back
spanning roughly Sept 8 – Oct 6.
