# Resort/Cruise calendar research

Researched to fill the gap flagged in `docs/EDITORIAL_CALENDAR.md`: only fashion-week
dates were documented, but the archive is now in a December window where cruise
coverage traditionally appears.

## Findings

Resort/Cruise collections run on two separated calendars:

- **Show window, roughly April–June.** Cruise 2026 examples: Chanel Apr 29 (Lake Como),
  Gucci May 15 (Florence), Louis Vuitton May 22 (Avignon), Dior May 27 (Rome), Max Mara
  Jun 17 (Caserta).
- **Retail window, roughly November–December.** Industry sources describe Resort/Cruise
  as landing in stores mid-November through December (timed to holiday shopping) and
  remaining at full price for an unusually long run (~6 months) versus other seasons.

This confirms the brief's premise: a December report seeing cruise-collection retail/
editorial coverage is an expected recurring pattern (echo of the spring shows), not
noise or a duplicate signal.

Added as a new section to `docs/EDITORIAL_CALENDAR.md`, alongside the existing
Sept–Oct fashion-week entry. No `data/reports/` files touched.
