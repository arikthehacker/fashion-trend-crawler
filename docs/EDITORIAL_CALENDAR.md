# Editorial Calendar — known recurring high-volatility windows

Reference for report-writing agents: check here before treating a volume/volatility
shift as anomalous or re-researching fashion week dates from scratch (see
`docs/agent-logs/fashion-week-calendar-research.md` and
`docs/agent-logs/editorial-calendar-design.md` for background). This list is a
living reference — extend it, don't re-derive it each run.

## Fashion month, SS27 collections (Sept–Oct 2026)

Structurally expected to end the current low-volatility stretch — not noise.

- **New York (NYFW)**: Sept 8–15, 2026
- **London (LFW)**: Sept 17–21, 2026
- **Milan (MFW)**: Sept 23–29, 2026
- **Paris (PFW)**: Sept 29 – Oct 6, 2026

Overall window: **Sept 8 – Oct 6, 2026**. Sequence (NY → London → Milan → Paris)
is consistent year over year.

## Resort/Cruise collections (runway: Apr–Jun; retail arrival: Nov–Dec)

Two distinct windows, both recurring annually, separate from the Sept–Oct fashion-week
calendar above. See `docs/agent-logs/resort-cruise-calendar-research.md` for sourcing.

- **Runway/show window (roughly April–June)**: major houses present Resort/Cruise
  collections in staged, often destination, shows well ahead of the season name —
  e.g. Cruise 2026 ran Chanel (Apr 29, Lake Como), Gucci (May 15, Florence), Louis
  Vuitton (May 22, Avignon), Dior (May 27, Rome), Max Mara (Jun 17, Caserta). Expect
  a comparable cluster of designer-origin signals roughly April–June each year.
- **Retail arrival window (roughly November–December)**: Resort/Cruise pieces
  typically hit stores mid-November through December, timed to holiday shopping,
  and stay on shop floors at full price for an unusually long ~6-month run. This is
  the window relevant to the archive right now — a December-dated report seeing
  cruise-collection coverage (retail/editorial framing of pieces shown back in
  spring) is an expected recurring pattern, not a bug or a stale/duplicate signal.

Sequence is show-first (spring), sell-first (fall/winter) — the same collection name
spans both windows six-plus months apart, which is a legitimate source of confusion
when reconciling signal dates against collection windows.
